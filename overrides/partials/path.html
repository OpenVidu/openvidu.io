Copy of mkdocs-material 9.7.6 partials/path.html, with two changes: 1. The trail starts at the product section ("OpenVidu Meet", "OpenVidu Platform") instead of the site homepage, which the header logo already links to. 2. A trail whose single item links to the page the visitor is already on is dropped: an ancestor renders as a link to its first leaf page (path-item.html), so a one-page section like "Pricing", or a product landing like "OpenVidu Meet", would otherwise render a trail pointing at itself. Deeper trails keep every ancestor, first-page-of-a-section included, so the trail never loses context.
